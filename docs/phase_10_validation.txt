Features and logic
- SHA-256 is calculated from original upload bytes before parsing and persisted as content_hash.
- Forensic Trust Score measures evidence actionability, not system safety:
  40% data quality + 30% average detection risk + 30% binary rule/ML agreement.
- Agreement counts both-engines-flagged and neither-engine-flagged logs as agreement.
